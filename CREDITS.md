# Credits

The generators in `tools/` are our own work (CC0), as is everything they make. They build from Poly Haven's CC0
sources, credited below although CC0 asks for no attribution: knowing where a file came from is what makes it safe to
ship. Every source file is also recorded, with its Poly Haven page, in the `assetkit-texture-sources` index.

The generators were written for Trail Trainer and moved here on 2026-10-14; the tables were Trail Trainer's.
